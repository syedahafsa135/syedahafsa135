I'm a Data Analyst with a Computer Systems Engineering background, currently pursuing my **Master's in Data Science at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM)**. I enjoy turning messy, real-world data into dashboards and reports that people actually use to make decisions.

My experience spans SQL-based analysis, Power BI/Tableau dashboarding, and end-to-end EDA — most recently as a Data Analyst Intern at Technocolabs Software, where I built dashboards and defined KPIs to track business outcomes.
